## 🫁 The Complete Organ Cards (24)
These are the vital body elements placed on the board. [1] 

* 
* Appendix: Basic organ; high priority target.
* Bladder: Fragile organ; easily targeted.
* Bones: Structural; targeted by breaks and degradation.
* Bowels: Lower digestive system; targeted by food mishaps.
* Brain: Nervous system control card.
* Esophagus: Upper food pipe.
* Eyes: Dual sensory structures.
* Gallbladder: Small organ prone to crystallizations.
* Heart: Central life element.
* Kidneys: Paired filtration organs.
* Liver: Large metabolic blood filter.
* Lungs: Paired respiratory air processors.
* Nose: Sensory facial organ.
* Pancreas: Glandular chemical processor.
* Skeletal Muscles: Motor movement tissues.
* Skin: Outer body barrier.
* Spleen: Lymphatic blood filter.
* Stomach: Primary digestion chamber.
* Teeth: Hard structures in the jaw.
* Thyroid: Metabolic hormone producer.
* Tongue: Oral sensory muscle.
* Tonsils: Throat-based immune guards.
* Trachea: Main respiratory windpipe.
* Wild Organ: Heavy asset; takes 4 afflictions to destroy. [2, 3, 4]

## 🃏 Tactical, Special, & Bureaucracy Cards (43 Total)
These cards alter the field rules, manipulate players' hands, counter incoming ailments, or save your organs from being discarded. [12] 
## Bureaucracy & Turn Manipulation

* 
* Botched Surgery!: Forces targeted players to swap, lose, or incorrectly apply clinical cards.
* By the Book!: Forces strict adherence to standard layout rules; neutralizes certain active deck modifications.
* Chart Mix-up: All players gather their current hands, shuffle them together, and deal them back out equally.
* Clinical Audit!: Forces all opponents to show you their hands; you pick one card from each to discard.
* Contagious! (3 copies): Play instantly out of turn to redirect an incoming affliction onto another player's identical organ.
* Cryopreservation: Freezes an organ card. It cannot be attacked or affected by any cards until thawed.
* Kidney Donor!: Special trading mechanic specifically dealing with clearing kidney health lines.
* Metastasis! (2 copies): Immediately copy and apply a just-played affliction onto another valid organ card.
* Outbreak! (3 copies): Spreads shared sicknesses across multiple boards simultaneously.
* Research! (2 copies): Allows you to dig deep into the draw pile or discard pile for specific resources.
* Sedate!: Forces an opponent to completely skip their next two consecutive gameplay turns.
* Transplant: Permanently steal one completely healthy organ from an opponent's body layout to add to your own. [7, 9, 10, 13, 14] 
* 

## Defense & Cure Cards

* 
* Immunity Boost! (7 copies): Standard instant counter-card. Play it out of turn to completely block any single attack or action card targeting you.
* Vaccine: Pre-emptive shield card placed onto an organ to make it completely safe from incoming standard infections.
* Medicine! (3 copies): Curative play that removes 1 active affliction counter from an injured organ.
* Medical Miracle!: Powerful healing card that completely cleanses all active afflictions from a single organ at once.
* It's Alive!: A rare recovery card that can bring back a recently destroyed organ from the graveyard. [9, 15, 16] 
* 

## Deadly Traps & Instant Attacks

* 
* POISONED!: An instant-trigger trap card. If you draw it from the deck, you must immediately play it and sacrifice one of your own healthy organs. [10] 
* Necrosis! (5 copies): The ultimate attack. Counts as 2 full afflictions at once, immediately killing any standard organ card on the spot. [10, 17]
